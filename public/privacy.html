1. Overview & Purpose of WForge

WForge is a student-led 5th grade 3D printing enterprise manufacturing school-friendly, durable, non-toxic PLA products, desk fidgets, and custom items. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information of our students, parents, and community members.

2. Information We Collect

When you use WForge or authenticate using Google Sign-In or email, we collect only the minimum required information:

  • Google Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture provided via Google OAuth to identify your account and associate your 3D print orders.
  • Email Address & Authentication Data: Used for account verification, password resets, and sending order status updates (pickup readiness and reward points notifications).
  • Preorder Information: Print preferences, color choices, redeemed reward points, and order history.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate you and provide secure access to your account and orders.
  • Process and manufacture your school 3D print requests.
  • Track earned reward points (+1 point = 1¢ discount off future orders).
  • Send notification emails from wforge10@gmail.com regarding order delivery and reward points.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties. We only integrate with trusted service providers essential for application functionality:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: For secure Google Sign-In and email verification.
  • Resend / Email Services: For dispatching transactional pickup notifications.

5. Data Security & Retention

Authentication credentials and session tokens are encrypted using industry-standard protocols through Google Firebase. You may request the deletion of your account and preorder data at any time by contacting us.
